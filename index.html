The Theme

Two ideas, one campus

Five centuries ago, the same hands that sketched cathedrals also charted the stars — refusing to separate the beautiful from the exact. Techfest borrows that instinct.

This edition sets the precision of a hackathon inside the ornament of an atelier, and the scale of orbital mechanics inside three days on a Mumbai campus. Robotics arenas sit beside speaker halls; drone races run under the same sky as an astronomy exhibit.

Est. 1998 — organised entirely by the student community of IIT Bombay.
